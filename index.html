About

The work
behind
the work.

Creative direction is not decoration. It is the difference between a campaign people forget and one that defines how an audience feels about a brand for years. And between a client you win once and a client who stays.

I work at the intersection of strategy and production: building the narrative first, then the experience around it.

Background

Swedish creative director with over 20 years building brand platforms, creative narratives, and live experiences. Started with Gothenburg Dance Festival, one of Scandinavia's largest urban expression competitions, with 10,000+ participants across eight consecutive years. Expanded into international brand programmes, live television, and national cultural projects funded by the Swedish government.

Approach

Sometimes the hardest part isn't finding a solution. It's finding the right problem to solve. Clients don't always know what they need, and teams don't always know what the client means. That gap is where projects quietly go wrong. So before I touch the concept, I ask questions until the problem, the goal, and the why are clear to everyone in the room. When that alignment happens, the work gets faster, sharper, and easier to stand behind. And clients come back.

Currently

Delivering "Early Access", a three-year international brand programme for Play'n GO, a global gaming company, across seven countries (Sweden, Spain, UK, Malta, Hungary, Poland, Philippines). Open to brand creative leadership roles in entertainment, gaming, and international live programmes. Based in Gothenburg.
