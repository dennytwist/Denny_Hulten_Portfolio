About

Built from
nothing.
Twice.

I build platforms from nothing and grow movements out of them. Co-founded Twisted Feet into the dominant cultural force for urban expression in western Sweden. Joined Eventity as a logistics event company and rebuilt it as a creative strategy agency. Third time's the charm.

Scale

Gothenburg Dance Festival: 10,000+ participants, eight years running. National government-funded tours. Play'n GO across seven countries. Productions with Veronica Maggio, Håkan Hellström, Petter, Molly Sandén, Cleo, Isak Danielson, Ace of Base, GRANT, Svante Randlert, Niklas Källner, and Olympic swimmer Therese Alshammar.

Approach

I work on the problem behind the brief. Most clients know what they want. Few know what they need. That gap is where the best work lives, and where projects quietly go wrong if nobody looks. I find it before anyone starts building.

Leadership

At Play'n GO: designed the delivery mechanism for their self-leadership culture. A three-level programme covering Lead Yourself, Lead Your Team, and Lead Your Function across 540 people in seven countries. Live data collection at every event, Pulse Report delivered to the steering group.

At Twisted Feet: built the cultural infrastructure that kept creative talent from leaving their region. Platforms, systems, and 18 years of developing practitioners who could make it where they were, not where they thought they had to go.

Currently

Creative Director at Eventity. Leading Play'n GO's three-year international programme across seven countries and a five-year game world for Länsförsäkringar headlined by Veronica Maggio. Open to creative leadership in gaming, entertainment, and large-scale live programmes. Based in Gothenburg, open to relocation.
