    Denny Hultén
    Creative Director

    I build.
    And rebuild.rebuild.

    Co-founded Twisted Feet, a street-dance company, and grew it from nothing into a movement: the dominant cultural force for urban expression in western Sweden. Helped rebuild Eventity from a logistics event company into a creative strategy agency.

    Scale

    Gothenburg Dance Festival: 10,000+ participants a year, eight editions. National government-funded theatre tours. Live productions with Veronica Maggio, Håkan Hellström, Petter, Cleo, and Ace of Base.

    Leadership

    At Play'n GO, I built the delivery mechanism for a self-leadership culture that lived on paper but never reached people: a behaviour-change programme now running across 540 employees. At Twisted Feet, I built the infrastructure that kept a generation of creative talent from leaving the region.

    Currently

    Creative Director at Eventity, leading Play'n GO's international programme and a five-year game world for Länsförsäkringar headlined by Veronica Maggio. Open to creative leadership in gaming, entertainment, and large-scale live work. Based in Gothenburg, open to relocation.
