# About Section: Raw Material Prompts

The about section works when it sounds like you and no one else.
These questions are meant to pull out the specific stuff — not credentials, not job titles, not "I bring clarity to complex problems."

---

What are two or three things that only you would say, that no other creative director could honestly claim?

Not your skills. Not what you do. Moments, habits, ways of seeing.
The stuff that sounds a little weird until you explain it, and then it clicks.

---

Some ways to get at it:

- What do you do in the first hour of a new project that most people don't do?
- What have you said to a client or a team that surprised them but turned out to be exactly right?
- What's a situation you've been in that no one would believe, and what did it teach you?
- What do you notice in a room that other people miss?
- When has your instinct been completely wrong, and what did you learn?

---

Bring the raw answers here. Don't polish them.
The polish comes after.
