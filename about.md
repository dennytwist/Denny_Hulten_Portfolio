# About — Denny Hultén

I help companies with the creative process. Starting with the why, and leading the work from there.

At Eventity that means building strategic events and campaigns around what each client is actually trying to do. A three-year international programme for Play'n GO, 540 people across seven countries. Länsförsäkringar, a five-year strategic framework built as a game world. National tours and productions with Petter, Molly Sandén, Ace of Base, Cleo, Svante Randlert, Niklas Källner, and Therese Alshammar.

The same instinct shaped eighteen years as a dancer and director at Gothenburg Dance Festival, building something that 2,000 students a year kept returning to. And the Volvo Cars productions across EMEA and APAC, where a show in front of 300 people needed to carry directly to Asia the following week.

I hold the full stack from strategy to stage. Not because I have to. Because that's where the concept either lives or dies.

Productions are a bit like dance. You don't always know what song will play. Twenty years in. Still dancing. Let's talk.
